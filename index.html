Our Story

About

Sheesh Mahal is a culmination of East and South Asian upbringings presented through the unmistakable sound of early 2000s Metal and Rock. Based out of Richmond, Ca, the band was formed in 2021 by ex Adjudicator guitarist, Joti Singh and guitarist Dustin Chinn. The two quickly bonded with Jazmin Hope Virility frontman Nuri "Blackjazz" Jones and drummer Vincenzo Blackdeer. After an initial string of shows, the band began to develop their unique sound which fans liken to such acts as System of a Down, Incubus, Queens of the Stone Age and Deftones. With their most recent release, "Mosquito" and new addition to bass, Jackson Maurer, Sheesh Mahal has solidified themselves as a highly anticipated act.
