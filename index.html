Our Story

About

Founded in 2021 in Richmond, California, Sheesh Mahal was formed by Joti Singh and Dustin Chinn with a vision of creating heavy music without limitations. Drummer Vince Blackdeer and bassist Nuri Jones joined shortly after, helping establish the band's presence throughout the Bay Area and Northern California metal scene. From the start, Sheesh Mahal set out to challenge expectations — rooted in metal, but drawing on jazz, classical, flamenco, and progressive music to build a sound that is technically ambitious and emotionally impactful.

That progression runs through the catalog, from early Bandcamp and SoundCloud demos to officially released singles on major streaming platforms — each one reflecting a growing willingness to experiment and push into new territory. In 2025, Jackson Maurer joined the band on bass, opening its next chapter. Every member is a multi-instrumentalist, keeping the band's sonic possibilities virtually endless. With numerous Bay Area shows behind them and new material in development, Sheesh Mahal continues building a reputation for dynamic live performance and fearless songwriting.
